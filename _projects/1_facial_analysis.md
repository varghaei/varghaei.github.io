Our ICCV 2025 Workshops paper, which received the **Best Student Paper** award, introduces an interpretable computer vision pipeline that measures facial change from photographs using 468-point landmarks, symmetry, perceived age, and face recognition. It was evaluated on more than 7,000 images from over 1,200 patients, and identity was preserved after surgery at a 99.5% true match rate {% cite varghaei2025automated %}.
