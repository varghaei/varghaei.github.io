2025

  1. ICCVW
    Automated Assessment of Aesthetic Outcomes in Facial Plastic Surgery
    Pegah Varghaei, Kiran Abraham-Aggarwal, Manoj T. Abraham, and Arun Ross
    In Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision Workshops (ICCVW), Oct 2025
    Best Student Paper Abs arXiv Bib HTML Code Poster

    Received the Best Student Paper award at the ICCV 2025 Workshops.

    A computer vision framework for quantifying aesthetic outcomes from real-world facial plastic surgery images using landmark-based symmetry, nasal measurements, perceived age, and identity-preservation analysis, evaluated on the largest curated dataset of its kind.

    Feng Liu, Ryan Ashbaugh, Nicholas Chimitt, Najmul Hassan, Ali Hassani, Ajay Jaiswal, Minchul Kim, Zhiyuan Mao, Christopher Perry, Zhiyuan Ren, Yiyang Su, Pegah Varghaei, Kai Wang, Xingguang Zhang, Stanley Chan, Arun Ross, Humphrey Shi, Zhangyang Wang, Anil Jain, and Xiaoming Liu
