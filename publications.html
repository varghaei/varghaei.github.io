Automated Assessment of Aesthetic Outcomes in Facial Plastic Surgery Best Student Paper
Pegah Varghaei, Kiran Abraham-Aggarwal, Manoj T. Abraham, Arun Ross
Proceedings of the IEEE/CVF International Conference on Computer Vision Workshops (ICCVW), 2025
A computer vision framework for quantifying aesthetic outcomes from real-world facial plastic surgery images using landmark-based symmetry, nasal measurements, perceived age, and identity-preservation analysis, evaluated on the largest curated dataset of its kind.
