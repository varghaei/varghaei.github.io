I am a PhD candidate in Computer Science and Engineering at Michigan State University, where I work on computer vision, biometrics, and generative AI. My research focuses on recognizing and analyzing people from images and video, including face and whole-body recognition at long range, and on generative and vision-language models for facial image analysis. I hold an MS in Mechanical Engineering and a BS in Computational Mathematics, both from Michigan State University.

My first-author paper at the ICCV 2025 Workshops received the Best Student Paper award. I am a coauthor of the FarSight whole-body biometric system (WACV 2024), developed under the IARPA BRIAR program, and I have three first-author manuscripts currently under review. I expect to graduate in December 2027 and am interested in research internships in computer vision and generative AI.

Research interests: computer vision · biometrics and face recognition · generative AI · vision-language models · trustworthy evaluation

Featured: Best Student Paper, ICCV 2025 Workshops

Automated Assessment of Aesthetic Outcomes in Facial Plastic Surgery

An interpretable computer vision pipeline that measures facial change from photographs using 468-point landmarks, symmetry, perceived age, and face recognition, evaluated on more than 7,000 images from over 1,200 patients. Identity was preserved after surgery at a 99.5% true match rate.
